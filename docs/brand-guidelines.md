# DAVID888 Daily Logo Guidelines

## Concept
B / 電台刊物 pairs an editorial serif `Daily` wordmark with the open Podcasts symbol. `David888` acts as the masthead, the divider creates a broadcast schedule rhythm, and `每日放送` anchors the Chinese name.

## Primary lockup
Use `public/brand/logo.svg` on paper or light neutral surfaces. Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the Podcasts symbol's center dot. The artwork is a stacked square lockup.

## Variants
- `logo.svg` / `logo-color.svg`: full-colour primary lockup.
- `logo-black.svg`: black on white.
- `logo-white.svg`: white on black or a calm dark surface.
- `podcasts.svg`: official Apache 2.0 Podcasts symbol source.
- `app/icon.png` and `public/icons/*`: digital app and favicon artwork.

## Palette

| Role | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#FFF7E9` | 255 247 233 | Primary background |
| Broadcast red | `#BF461D` | 191 70 29 | Primary mark and type |
| Ink | `#000000` | 0 0 0 | One-colour print |
| Reverse | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | Dark backgrounds |

Approximate print conversion: Broadcast red CMYK 0 63 85 25. Confirm against a physical proof before print production.

## Minimum sizes
Use the full lockup at 96 px or wider in page headers. Use the app icon at 48 px or larger when the brand name must be read. At 16 px, the Podcasts silhouette is the recognition cue; the text remains present by requirement but is not expected to be legible.

## Misuse
Do not stretch, squash, rotate, recolour outside the palette, add shadows or gradients, rearrange the lockup, or place the mark on a busy background without a calm container.
